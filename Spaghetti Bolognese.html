Spaghetti Bolognese Recipe


About This Recipe

Our Spaghetti Bolognese is made with perfectly cooked pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned ground beef, and aromatic herbs. Every bite delivers authentic Italian flavor that is both hearty and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti Pasta

  • Ground Beef

  • Tomato Sauce

  • Onion

  • Garlic

  • Olive Oil

  • Italian Herbs

  • Parmesan Cheese

  • Fresh Basil

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti until al dente.

  2. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil.

  3. Add ground beef and cook well.

  4. Stir in tomato sauce and herbs.

  5. Mix with the cooked spaghetti.

  6. Serve with Parmesan and basil.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

Difficulty : Medium
