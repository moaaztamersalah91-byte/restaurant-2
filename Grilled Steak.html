    Grilled Steak Recipe


    About This Recipe

    Our Grilled Steak is cooked to perfection with premium beef, seasoned with aromatic herbs and spices. Grilled over high heat to lock in its juicy flavor, this steak is served with fresh vegetables and crispy fries.

    Ingredients

    • Beef Steak

    • Olive Oil

    • Salt

    • Black Pepper

    • Garlic

    • Butter

    • Fresh Rosemary

    • Fresh Thyme

    • French Fries

    Preparation Steps

    1. Season the steak with salt and pepper.

    2. Brush with olive oil and herbs.

    3. Grill over high heat on both sides.

    4. Add butter and garlic while cooking.

    5. Cook to your preferred doneness.

    6. Serve hot with fries and vegetables.

    Recipe Information

    Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

    Difficulty : Medium
